1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

Kabadiwala Connect is a next-generation circular economy digital platform 
designed to formalize, streamline, and secure the e-waste and scrap collection 
ecosystem in India. 

The Problem:
In India, over 90% of electronic waste (e-waste) is handled by the unorganized, 
informal sector (local kabadiwalas and scrap aggregators). Informal collectors 
often lack:
- Transparent, real-time scrap market pricing.
- Accurate technical classification of complex e-waste components (e.g., PCBs, 
  lithium-ion batteries, optical sensors, copper windings).
- Direct access to authorized, government-certified recyclers (MPCB/CPCB).
- Binding price guarantees against arbitrary deductions during pickup.
- Safety guidelines to protect themselves against toxic hazards (lead, mercury, 
  cadmium, battery acid).
- Legitimate documentation needed for Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) 
  compliance mandated by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.

The Solution:
Kabadiwala Connect bridges the gap between individual households, informal 
collectors, aggregators, and government-authorized formal recycling centers. 
Equipped with computer vision AI for instant multi-item scrap valuation, 
per-material rate bidding with real-time anomaly detection, binding quote-lock 
guarantees, gated dispatch controls, interactive GPS tracking, automated Form 6 
statutory manifest generation, six structured data schemas, and multilingual 
voice-first accessibility, it transforms e-waste recycling into a transparent, 
profitable, and compliant operation.

2. TECHNOLOGY STACK & ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Frontend Architecture:
- Framework: Next.js (App Router, React 19 / React 18, TypeScript)
- Styling: Modern TailwindCSS & CSS3 with high-contrast, accessible palettes,
  glassmorphic cards, responsive grid systems, and subtle micro-animations.
- Icons & Visuals: Lucide React, Canvas Confetti for transaction milestones.
- Multilingual Audio / TTS: Native Web Speech API integrated with custom 
  multilingual translations across 4 Indian languages.
- Document Generation: jsPDF and jsPDF-AutoTable for client-side generation 
  of legally compliant Form 6 EPR Certificates.
- Data Export: Built-in Blob-based CSV export engine for all 6 structured e-waste datasets.

Backend Architecture:
- Framework: FastAPI (Python 3.9+) with asynchronous request handling and CORS.
- AI / Deep Learning: YOLOv8 (Ultralytics) running on a custom-trained model 
  weights (`best.pt`) optimized for electronic scrap components.
- Image Processing: Pillow (PIL) and NumPy for bounding box spatial calculations 
  and multi-object segmentation.
- Fallback Heuristics: Secondary classification engine that analyzes visual 
  and density features if YOLO confidence is sub-threshold or scrap is heavily mixed.

3. DUAL-USER PERSONA SYSTEM (SELLER VS RECYCLER)
================================================================================

The application provides two dedicated, persona-tailored interfaces accessible 
from the Role Selection screen or switched dynamically from the header:

1. Collector & Aggregator / Household Seller Portal ('user'):
   - Designed with high-accessibility, intuitive cards, large click targets, and 
     read-aloud voice support for informal kabadiwalas and household sellers.
   - Core capabilities: AI camera scanning, scrap valuation, reviewing locked quotes 
     with per-material breakdowns, accepting/declining quotes, choosing between 
     doorstep pickup or center drop-off, selecting certified recyclers, tracking 
     pickup drivers live, verifying digital scale weights, and collecting digital 
     or cash payments.

2. Authorized Recycler Partner Portal ('recycler'):
   - Designed for formal e-waste dismantling facilities (e.g., GreenCycle 
     Recycling, authorized by state pollution control boards).
   - Core capabilities: Live incoming scrap lot queue, per-material buying rate 
     quotation with real-time anomaly detection, instant subtotal/grand total 
     calculation, quote-locking, gated driver dispatch (enabled only upon collector 
     acceptance), verifying physical weights on calibrated scales, updating daily 
     buying rate cards, downloading Form 6 Manifests & EPR certificates, maintaining 
     compliance audit logs, and exploring/exporting the six structured e-waste datasets.

4.4. Per-Material Rate Entry & Real-Time Anomaly Detection
----------------------------------------------------------
- In the Recycler Dashboard (`IncomingLotsTab.tsx` / `QuoteRatesModal.tsx`), recyclers 
  can quote individual, itemized rates for every material detected in a lot.
- Reference District Median Rates:
  * Laptops: ₹520/kg
  * Smartphones: ₹450/kg
  * PCBs / Circuit boards: ₹355/kg
  * Digital cameras: ₹350/kg
  * Copper cables: ₹210/kg
  * Motors & compressors: ₹140/kg
  * Keyboards: ₹115/kg
  * Optical mice: ₹105/kg
  * Lithium batteries: ₹75/kg
  * Display panels: ₹85/kg
  * Mixed e-waste: ₹180/kg
- Real-Time Rate Anomaly Feedback:
  * If a recycler enters a rate more than 15% below the district average (`rate < median * 0.85`), 
    an amber warning box displays immediately:
    "⚠ This rate is ₹[X] below the district average of ₹[median]/kg. Collector will be notified of the below-market offer."
  * If the entered rate is above the district median (`rate > median`), a green checkmark displays:
    "Above market rate — competitive offer."
  * If within 15% of the median, no extra warning is shown.
  * Note: The warning is informational and does not block submission.
- Calculation & Persistence:
  * Subtotal per line item = `Rate (₹/kg) × Declared Weight (kg)`
  * Grand Total Committed Quote (₹)
  * Confirmed quotes are recorded in `material_transactions` schema with fields: 
    `lot_id`, `material_category`, `weight_kg`, `rate_per_kg`, `subtotal_inr`, `recycler_id`, `timestamp`.

4.5. Quote-Lock Mechanism & Rate Guarantee Protocol
---------------------------------------------------
- After the recycler submits the per-material rates, the lot is transitioned to 
  the status **`Quote Locked`** (`quoteStatus: 'locked'`).
- The quoted unit rates are stored immutably against the lot ID.
- Status Badge Styling:
  * **Quote Locked**: Styled with a green/success background badge (`bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border-emerald-200`) 
    featuring a lock icon and text: `"Quote Locked · ₹[total amount]"` (calculated from `quotedRates`).
  * **Quote Declined**: Styled with an amber/warning background badge (`bg-amber-50 text-amber-700 border-amber-200`) 
    with text: `"Quote Declined — Finding New Recycler"`.
- Seller Side Experience (`LotDetailsScreen.tsx`):
  * Displays a dedicated read-only locked quote breakdown table detailing each 
    material, declared weight, unit rate committed by the recycler, and subtotal payout.
  * Two prominent action buttons:
    1. **"Accept Quote"**: Locks in the agreement, sets `quoteStatus: 'accepted'`, 
       and enables doorstep pickup dispatch.
    2. **"Decline and Find Another Recycler"**: Resets the lot status to `Finding Recycler` 
       (`quoteStatus: 'declined'`), allowing the collector to choose a different recycler.
- Binding Rate Guarantee:
  * Once accepted, the unit rates (₹/kg) cannot be altered by the recycler.
  * Physical scale weigh-in during pickup only adjusts the weight quantity, 
    strictly honoring the locked unit rates.

4.6. Interactive GPS Geolocation & Address Picker
-------------------------------------------------
- Integrated interactive GPS map picker (`InteractiveGPSMapPicker`).
- Automatically detects current GPS coordinates with accuracy circle display.
- Allows users to drag the marker or tap on the map to pinpoint exact scrap 
  storage or pickup location.
- Reverse geocoding resolves latitude and longitude into street, landmark, city, 
  and PIN code.

4.7. Dispatch Management, Gated Authorization & Real-Time Driver Tracking
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Gated Dispatch Authorization:
  * On each incoming lot card in the Recycler Dashboard, the "Notify Pickup Person On The Way" 
    button is **disabled and visually grayed out** if `lot.quoteStatus !== 'accepted'`.
  * Helper tooltip/text below the disabled button explains:
    "Complete rate entry and await collector acceptance before dispatching."
  * Once the collector clicks "Accept Quote", the dispatch button activates immediately.
- Driver Assignment & En-Route Notification:
  * The recycler inputs driver name, phone, vehicle number, and ETA minutes.
  * The seller receives a live notification banner: "Pickup Partner En Route" with 
    countdown timer, driver contact, vehicle plate, and live route tracking.

4.8. Verified Handover, Calibrated Weighing & Instant Settlement
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Step-by-step physical handover protocol to eliminate cheating and price disputes.
- 3-Way Weight Verification:
  1. Estimated Weight (AI estimated).
  2. Seller's Declared Weight.
  3. Verified Weight measured on the certified digital scale at handover.
- Weight Discrepancy Calculator: Automatically recomputes final payout based on 
  certified scale readings using the immutable locked rates.
- Digital OTP / Security PIN: Handover requires exchanging a unique 4-digit PIN.
- Payment Mode: Instant Cash Payout or Direct UPI/Bank Transfer with transaction 
  reference ID.

4.9. Six Structured E-Waste Datasets & CSV Export Engine
--------------------------------------------------------
- Located under the **"Datasets"** tab in the Recycler Dashboard (`DatasetsTab.tsx`).
- Provides 6 relational dataset schemas populated with realistic Indian e-waste 
  mock data with working **"Download CSV"** buttons that generate exact filenames:
  1. `material_dataset.csv`: Physical & chemical taxonomy, hazard levels, critical 
     mineral contents (Li/Co/Nd/Ta/Ga/In), and EPR applicability.
  2. `price_dataset.csv`: District-level time-series buying rates, transaction sample 
     counts, week start dates, and trend directions.
  3. `recycler_dataset.csv`: SPCB/MPCB license numbers, CPCB registrations, facility 
     GPS coordinates, accepted material categories, and authorization statuses.
  4. `transaction_traceability_dataset.csv`: Complete chain of custody logs with 
     dual weights, rate/kg, photo hashes, GPS coordinates, timestamps, and Form 6 manifests.
  5. `collector_dataset.csv`: Demographics, preferred vernacular languages, phone hashes, 
     volumes recycled, and lifetime earnings.
  6. `ml_training_dataset.csv`: Computer vision training records with YOLO predictions, 
     human corrections, confidence scores, and feedback sources.

5. STEP-BY-STEP FLOWS & USABILITY
================================================================================

5.1. Household / Collector Seller Flow (End-to-End)
---------------------------------------------------
1. Language Onboarding:
   - Select preferred language (English, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi) with voice previews.
2. Select Seller Role:
   - Select "Collector & Aggregator / Household Seller".
3. AI Camera Scan & Valuation:
   - Tap "Sell Material", snap photo of scrap.
   - AI detects components, creates bounding boxes, estimates weight and value.
4. Logistics & Recycler Selection:
   - Choose "Doorstep Pickup" (set GPS pin) or "Self Drop-off".
   - Select preferred authorized recycler (e.g., GreenCycle Recycling).
5. Quote-Lock Review & Acceptance:
   - When recycler submits per-material rates, lot status becomes `Quote Locked`.
   - Seller reviews the itemized rate breakdown table.
   - Seller clicks "Accept Quote" (or "Decline and Find Another Recycler").
6. Live Pickup Tracking:
   - Once accepted, recycler dispatches driver; seller tracks live vehicle route and ETA.
7. Physical Handover & Settlement:
   - Driver verifies weight on certified scale, calculates final payout based 
     on the immutable locked rate, enters 4-digit PIN, and completes payment.
8. Earnings & EPR Manifest:
   - Wallet credited with payout; Form 6 EPR certificate ready for download.

5.2. Authorized Recycler Operational Flow (End-to-End)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Access Recycler Dashboard:
   - Switch role to "Authorized Recycler Partner".
2. Review Incoming Lots:
   - Navigate to "Lots" tab; review active, in-transit, and pending lots.
3. Per-Material Quoting & Anomaly Validation:
   - Click "Quote Per-Material Rates & Lock" on an incoming lot.
   - Enter ₹/kg buying rate for each detected material item.
   - System checks rate against district median in real-time (shows warnings/badges).
   - Review auto-calculated subtotal and grand total.
   - Click "Lock Quote & Submit" (saves to `material_transactions` and notifies seller).
4. Gated Dispatching:
   - While quote is pending review, the dispatch button remains disabled.
   - Once seller clicks "Accept Quote", click "Notify Pickup Person On The Way".
   - Enter driver name, phone, vehicle number, and ETA minutes.
5. Scale Verification & Handover:
   - Enter verified physical scale weight; system reconciles final payout.
6. EPR Compliance & Form 6 Manifest:
   - Generate, preview, and download statutory Form 6 PDF certificate.
7. Explore Datasets:
   - Switch to "Datasets" tab to inspect and export all 6 structured e-waste datasets as CSV.

7.1. TypeScript Data Models (`src/types/lot.ts`)

```typescript
export type LotStatus =
  | 'Draft'
  | 'Ready for Recycler'
  | 'Recycler Selected'
  | 'Pickup Scheduled'
  | 'Pickup Person On The Way'
  | 'Handed Over'
  | 'Handover Pending'
  | 'Payment Pending'
  | 'Completed'
  | 'Quote Locked'
  | 'Quote Declined'
  | 'Finding Recycler';

export interface MaterialQuote {
  material_category: string;
  weight_kg: number;
  rate_per_kg: number;
  subtotal_inr: number;
  recycler_id?: string;
  timestamp?: string;
}

export interface MaterialTransaction {
  lot_id: string;
  material_category: string;
  weight_kg: number;
  rate_per_kg: number;
  subtotal_inr: number;
  recycler_id: string;
  timestamp: string;
}

export interface Lot {
  id: string;
  material?: string;
  weight?: number;
  priceRange?: string;
  offerPrice?: number;
  pickupType?: 'home' | 'self' | 'both';
  recycler?: any;
  status: LotStatus;
  timestamp?: string;
  quotedRates?: MaterialQuote[];
  quoteStatus?: 'pending' | 'locked' | 'accepted' | 'declined';
  quotedTotal?: number;
  verifiedWeight?: number;
  finalAmount?: number;
  customerAddress?: string;
  dispatchInfo?: any;
}
```

8. REGULATORY & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE (EPR & E-Waste Rules)
================================================================================

Under the E-Waste (Management) Rules established by the Ministry of Environment, 
Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC):
1. Producers must fulfill Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates via certified recyclers.
2. All e-waste transfers must maintain an official Form 6 Manifest trail.
3. Kabadiwala Connect enforces compliance through:
   - Verified SPCB/CPCB license verification.
   - Transparent per-material weight and rate logging.
   - Tamper-evident Form 6 digital manifests.
   - Quantified environmental impact metrics (CO₂ diverted, heavy metals captured).
