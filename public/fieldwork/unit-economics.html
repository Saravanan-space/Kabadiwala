3. Platform Business Sustainability

The platform is completely free for informal scrap collectors to prevent compliance burden. Revenue is generated sustainably from the formal recycling industry:

1. Recycler EPR Traceability Fee

Authorized recyclers pay 2.5% – 3.0% on verified lots to receive GPS-tagged, photo-audited digital dossiers for statutory CPCB EPR compliance.

2. Bulk Logistics Batching Surcharge

A 1.5% routing fee on clustered bulk dispatches from local scrap godowns, cutting transport costs by 35%.

Unit Economics per 50kg Lot: Gross Lot Value = ₹11,500 | Platform 3% Recycler Fee = ₹345 | Server & ML inference cost = -₹5.20 → Net Platform Contribution = +₹339.80 / lot (83%+ Gross Margin).
