Ground Visit Findings & Market Insights

During our field visit on 21/09/2026 to N.R.A Scrap Merchant in Bikasipura, Bengaluru, we investigated ground realities regarding the integration of informal scrap collectors into the formal Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) e-waste recycling ecosystem through a vernacular, low-literacy, offline-first mobile platform. Our interaction revealed that a large multi-tier supply chain currently operates the scrap market—from street hawkers to small aggregators and large regional wholesalers—and the market is rapidly shifting online, with nearly 90% of his transactions and payments already conducted digitally via UPI and mobile channels. Additionally, he explained that low-weight, low-density materials (such as cardboard, plastics, and small components) cannot be sold easily or economically on a daily basis, forcing merchants to accumulate and store these items for several weeks before bulk dispatch. Overall, the merchant expressed high enthusiasm and confirmed that he is genuinely open to using our platform to access real-time price discovery, connect directly with authorized recyclers, optimize material aggregation, and streamline formal compliance.
